# Four-week field pilot

Start after deployment and participant-device readiness checks pass. Recruit 10–15 students with varied access needs, 3–5 teachers/therapists and participating parents. Use at least three short assignments per student per week.

Before week one, document consent, retention/deletion date and incident owner. Record Android model, OS/browser, assistive hardware/software, input mode and dwell settings. Verify the complete workflow per configuration. Observe each student's existing tools as a baseline before comparable app assignments.

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline, supported onboarding and parent-link/access-setting checks |
| 2 | Comparable classroom/home work; record corrections and blockers |
| 3 | Repeat assignments with less interface prompting where comfortable; retain teaching support |
| 4 | Repeat measurements, collect participant feedback and export results |

For each observation record student, phase, input mode, interface help, teaching help, additional worksheet/editor apps and technical blocker. Exclude established assistive tools from app-switch counts. Time capture through approved assignment including corrections. Time marking per short exercise separately from reading and recording feedback. Keep a pseudonymous incident log linking blocked assignments to their resolution. No background camera, microphone or gaze recording.

## Targets

- At least 80% of assigned work reaches review without a technical blocker; reconcile lifecycle counts with the incident log.
- At least 30% reduction in each student's median interface interventions versus their own baseline in the same input mode; report zero-baseline cases separately.
- At least 90% of sessions require no extra worksheet/editor app.
- Median preparation no more than 180 seconds, including corrections.
- Median marking interaction no more than 10 seconds per short exercise, excluding reading and recording.
- At least 70% of shared assignments acknowledged within 48 hours; separate open response windows.
- No unresolved loss of acknowledged saved work, cross-student access defect or blocking navigation failure in tested configurations.

Report sample counts, missing observations and outcomes by input mode from the export. Separate interface from teaching help throughout. Do not infer causation from this small observational pilot. Investigate lost saves or unauthorized access and verify a fix before resuming the affected configuration.
